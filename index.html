Scoring guide

How to score yourself

Score how each area felt today, not how it should feel or how your best week went. A quick gut rating beats a perfect one. If an area didn't come up, leave it blank.

1–3Struggling. It's draining you or getting in the way.
4–5Getting by. Neglected or a bit off.
6–7Okay. Some attention, mostly steady.
8–10Thriving. Cared for, and it's giving back.

Based on the Six Dimensions of Wellness Model by Dr. Bill Hettler (National Wellness Institute) and the Eight Dimensions of Wellness by Dr. Margaret Swarbrick, which adds Environmental and Financial. Cues are general examples, not a clinical assessment.

Tip: if you're stuck between two numbers, pick the lower one, then write one line in the day's note about what would move it up. Patterns across the week matter more than any single score.
